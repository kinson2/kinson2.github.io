Fela Fest is back and fresh as ever. In honor of Fela Kuti's birthday, October 15, we will play his music and other afrobeat artists in celebration of Africa's continued contribution to music that's spread across the globe.

This year's Fela Fest is Saturday, October 25, 2025, 9:00 pm at Gallery Art Bar! 

DJ MauMau and I are spinning and we may have other DJs join in on the fun.
