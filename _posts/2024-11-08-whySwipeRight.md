This research was a collaboration with Florian Berens (), Kelly Findley (University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign), Nicola Justice (Pacific Northwest), and I . We wanted to know more about statistics students ability to navigate the disciplines of statistics and data science. Do stat students see themselves in the discipline? Do our students identify with the work of statisticians and data scientists beyond graduation? What does it meant to them to feel as though they belong here? These kinds of questions - among others - are what we wanted to explore through a longitudinal study of statistics students as they matriculate at University of Illinois. Our reseaerch was elucidated in a journal article form and accepted as of this week.
